Acoustic piano notes rendered locally with MuseScore Studio 4.7.5 using its
Grand Piano instrument (MusicXML MIDI program 1), four-second whole notes at
quarter = 60. MIDI pitches 36 through 96 in steps of four semitones.

Files are mono 22050 Hz PCM16 WAV, peak-normalized to 0.65. Playback chooses
the nearest sample and changes its playback rate by the required semitones.
The notes are mixed and scheduled locally in Web Audio. They are not a
prerecorded accompaniment, and no score is sent to an external service.
